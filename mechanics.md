Core mechanics (the short version)
Three health bars: HP (Iron-scaled), Fatigue (Vigor-scaled), Stress (flat capacity, behavior-scaled rates). Stress reduction sources are apartment comfort, social interaction, substances (addiction risk), rest. The game forces human rhythms.

Four core stats: Faction Rep, Social Clout, Money (Dirham + Yuan), Signal (upload/download bandwidth).

Eight chargen stats: opposition pairs (Wire↔Shade, Grit↔Face, Wit↔Iron) + independent axis (Rigor, Vigor).

Skill totals: 18 skills across Combat (Blade, Brawl, Barrel, Surge, Venom), Dodge (Evade, Slip), Hacking (Spoof, Entry, Trace), Stealth (Prowl, Cover), Medical (Medic), Craft (Flash, Forge, Rig, Scrap), Social (Hustle, Menace). Each is a sum of two chargen stats. Vigor and Rigor never appear in skill totals — they modify accuracy.

Player org tiers: Team → Gang → Group → Coalition → Corp → Fund. The first four ship in Phase 1–2. Corp and Fund deferred until economy matures.

Clone system: death resets to last snapshot, costs Dirham, corpse remains in world (lootable). The clone-contract is the inherited Boundless covenant. The “help kms” command returns “If only it were that simple…” — this is the consent surface where players who dig find out they did not sign their own contract.

Economy: Dirham (in-world) + Yuan (premium, one-way purchase, no official cashout). EVE-style player-to-player trading. NPC factions tax the trades. Faction wealth feeds the World Model NN — rich faction = maintained district, poor faction = decay and freedom.

Two fixed NPC factions: the Royal Family (ceiling) and the Police (district-tax-funded enforcement). Everything else — Triads, businesses, gangs, religious orgs — procedurally named and managed.
