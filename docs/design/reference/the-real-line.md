# The Real LINE — Build Facts and Divergence Point

What actually exists in June 2026, so LINE IN THE SAND's alternate timeline knows what it's departing from.

---

## The Vision (announced January 2021)

Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman publicly unveiled THE LINE as the centerpiece of NEOM, the $500 billion megacity in Tabuk Province (northwestern Saudi Arabia, Red Sea coast extending into the interior desert). The original specification:

- **170 km long** — running from the Red Sea coast deep into the Saudi interior
- **500 meters tall** — taller than the Empire State Building
- **200 meters wide** — two parallel mirrored cliff-faces with the city between them
- **2.1 million square meters of mirrored glass cladding** on the exterior
- **9 million residents at full capacity**
- **260,000 people per square kilometer** density — roughly six times denser than Manila, the densest large city on Earth today
- **Zero cars, zero streets, zero carbon** — vertical and horizontal transport only, no surface roads
- **End-to-end transit in 20 minutes** via high-speed underground rail
- **AI managing everything** — climate control, transport scheduling, security, infrastructure maintenance, individual resource allocation
- **Digital twin** of the entire city running in real time
- **100% renewable energy** — solar plus green hydrogen
- **Targeted first habitable sections by 2030**, full completion 2045

The architectural plan was developed by Morphosis Architects (Los Angeles, Thom Mayne). Conceptual phase included Mark Foster Gage, Rem Koolhaas, Bjarke Ingels Group. The structural engineering was contracted to Bechtel, AECOM, Fluor, Hill International, with Samsung C&T, Hyundai E&C, and Archirodon as primary contractors. Major tunnel-boring contracts went to Italian and Korean consortia.

The wider NEOM project included Oxagon (floating industrial port and green hydrogen plant), Trojena (mountain ski resort hosting 2029 Asian Winter Games), Sindalah (luxury Red Sea island), and Magna (Red Sea coastal resorts).

## The Reality (June 2026)

**Construction officially suspended 16 September 2025** by Public Investment Fund governor Yasir Al Rumayyan, pending a strategic review. As of June 2026, work has not resumed.

What was actually built before suspension:
- **2.4 km of foundation work** along the eastern segment near Sharma
- Massive earthworks and excavation for the underground transit tunnels
- Several worker camps with capacity for ~140,000 laborers
- NEOM Bay Airport (operational for project workforce, limited charter)
- A few showpiece prefabricated structural mockups visible from the Red Sea coast

What got cancelled in early 2026:
- Eversendai structural steel contract for Trojena (March 2026)
- Webuild's contract for three Trojena dams (March 2026)
- Hyundai E&C / Samsung C&T / Archirodon tunnel contract for THE LINE
- Multiple Bechtel and AECOM contracts not renewed

What changed in the numbers:
- Population target 2030 slashed from 1.5 million to fewer than 300,000
- Internal audit leaked to *Wall Street Journal*: full completion projected to cost $8.8 trillion and stretch to 2080
- PIF write-down: $8 billion in 2025
- Total NEOM spending to date: $40-60 billion (vs. $500 billion+ originally allocated)
- PIF construction contracts dropped from $71 billion to $30 billion as money was reallocated to FIFA 2034 stadiums and Expo 2030
- Trojena Asian Winter Games postponed indefinitely (January 2026)

What survived the contraction:
- Oxagon green hydrogen plant — 80% complete, $8.4 billion total
- Sindalah island resort — 75-80% complete, late 2026 opening targeted
- Trojena ski resort — 35-40% complete, no firm timeline
- $5 billion DataVolt partnership for AI data center infrastructure
- $2.7 billion Hexagon AI infrastructure contract (US firms losing construction work, US tech firms capturing strategic AI work)

The pivot is from "futuristic residential megacity" to "industrial energy and AI infrastructure with luxury tourism appendages." The mirrored 170 km city is functionally dead. The 2.4 km foundation in the desert is a monument to it.

## The Labor

NEOM has been built by predominantly South Asian migrant workers under conditions documented by ALQST, Human Rights Watch, FairSquare, and the *Wall Street Journal*.

- **21,000 reported worker deaths** since Vision 2030 construction began in 2017, primarily Indian, Bangladeshi, Pakistani, and Nepali laborers (ITV documentary *Kingdom Uncovered: Inside Saudi Arabia*, October 2024)
- **100,000+ workers reported as "missing" or unaccounted for** across Vision 2030 megaprojects
- **84-hour work weeks** documented
- **Wage theft, passport confiscation, and inability to leave** without paying multi-month salaries to employers
- Workers describing themselves as "trapped slaves" and "beggars"
- Documented incidents of gang rape, attempted murder, and suicide in NEOM medical files
- Saudi government claims 1.12 deaths per 100,000 workers, disputes ITV figures
- Official 2023 count: 8 NEOM workplace deaths (vehicle crashes, collapsed tunnels and walls, mishandling of explosives)

Named cases include:
- **Abdul Wali Skandar Khan**, 25, Pakistani, killed 28 December 2023
- An unnamed China Communications Services worker killed by a falling metal gate
- 650 unexplained Nepali worker deaths flagged by Nepal's foreign employment board

Executive culture: Australian NEOM executive Wayne Borg was recorded calling South Asian workers "fucking morons" and saying "white people are at the top of the pecking order." NEOM CEO Nadhmi Al-Nasr departed in October-November 2024 amid the documentation.

The kafala (sponsorship) system has been formally reformed but workers remain bound to their employer for visa purposes and risk criminal "absconding" charges if they leave.

## The Indigenous Displacement

The Huwaitat tribe had inhabited the Tabuk region for centuries before NEOM. The Saudi state has forcibly displaced 20,000+ Huwaitat members from their lands. Resistance has been criminalized:

- **Abdul Rahim al-Huwaiti** was killed by Saudi security forces in April 2020 after publicly refusing to evacuate
- His cousins **Atallah, Ibrahim, and Abdullah al-Huwaiti** were sentenced to death in 2022 for protesting forced eviction; sentences upheld 2024
- Multiple other Huwaitat members imprisoned, sentenced to long prison terms, or executed

The land that THE LINE is being built on was taken from a people who lived there for generations. The empty desert in the renders is not empty. It was emptied.

## The Divergence Point for LINE IN THE SAND

In our timeline: PIF ran out of money, suspended construction, and pivoted to AI infrastructure. The Vision 2030 dream survived only as airports, hydrogen plants, and stalled foundations.

In LINE IN THE SAND's timeline: **淼رسومالعهود (Miǎo Rusum al-Uhud, "The Boundless Fees of the Covenants")** stepped in. A Chinese-Arab cloning company offering housing, medical care, and body-restoration in perpetuity to anyone who would sign their descendants into the contract. They financed the completion of THE LINE between roughly 2030 and 2055. They got it built because they had a labor model that doesn't run out: clone the workers, restore them on death, charge it against the family contract for four generations forward. The Saudi state took a 49% equity stake. The Huwaitat displacement continued and accelerated; their descendants now live four generations into the same covenant.

The LINE was completed at the full 170 km specification, populated, and has been inhabited for 70+ years by the time the game opens. The originals — the great-grandparents who signed the contracts in their forties — are still alive, brought back from vats each death, and most degraded by accumulated restoration distortion. The Boundless's corporate chop, 淼 in deep red inside a lacquer-black circle, appears on every door, every uniform, every piece of equipment, every contract. The mosque blue fractal geometry is on every covenant building's facade. The royals are untouchable. The police are district-tax-funded. The Triads run trade.

That's the alternate-history premise. Everything physical about THE LINE — 170 km, 500 m, 200 m, mirrored exterior, vertical stratification, AI management, end-to-end rail — is the announced 2021 specification, taken as if it had actually been built. Everything sociopolitical — the labor force, the indigenous displacement, the kafala-style contract binding, the multipolar Asian-Gulf capital — is extrapolated from what already happened in 2017-2026.

The Boundless is the fictional element. Everything else is documentation.
